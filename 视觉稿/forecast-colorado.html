Local FAQ

Can you see the northern lights from Denver?

Usually not from the city. You need a dark north horizon. Fort Collins area is the headline, not downtown Denver.

Is it better in the mountains?

Only if they do not block the north sky. A south-facing slope is worse than the eastern plains.
