## Usual destinations (does not vanish on a quiet night)

**Oval most nights.** Alaska Interior (Fairbanks area), northern Minnesota, Michigan’s Upper Peninsula, and northern Maine sit closer to the auroral oval. They still have clouds, twilight, and no-show nights. They are the default US places people travel for aurora, not a promise for tonight.

**Event nights only.** Colorado, Ohio, Illinois / Chicago, Indiana, and similar mid-latitude states need a strong storm. Most nights are no. When they go, the useful sky is usually the northern part of the state, away from the biggest light dome — not downtown.
