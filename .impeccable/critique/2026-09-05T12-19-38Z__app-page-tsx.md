### Design Specificity Verdict

The result feels authored for a practical aurora decision tool rather than a generic weather dashboard. The flat twilight band, oversized word verdict, dark decision card, and quiet paper body express the product's three-second decision model. The D.2 work strengthens that language without adding gradients, imagery, glass, or a second visual system.

Deterministic scan: `detect.mjs --json` returned `[]` across the changed markup and styles. No reliable browser overlay was injected because the available browser surface did not expose mutable script injection; desktop and 390px screenshots plus DOM/scroll-width checks were used instead.

### Overall Impression

The hierarchy now reads in the intended order: question, place input, verdict, then evidence. The strongest remaining product-truth risk is not visual: the current source snapshot can expose a NO answer sentence next to an UNKNOWN status.

### What's Working

1. The verdict card is denser and more authoritative while preserving the brief's 52/64 status scale and semantic order.
2. The in-band search reads as one control and retains a visible white focus treatment and 44px targets.
3. Forecast Nearby now exposes each destination's status and best window, making the rail useful before navigation.

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Snapshot answer/status mismatch**
  - Why it matters: A NO answer beside UNKNOWN can undermine trust in the decision.
  - Fix: Reconcile snapshot field consistency in the data layer in a separate engine-scoped change.
  - Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`
- **[P2] Mobile header omits primary navigation**
  - Why it matters: Returning users must rely on the footer for Tonight, Near me, and Guides.
  - Fix: Add an explicitly scoped mobile navigation pattern later.
  - Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`
- **[P2] All-stale table repeats UNKNOWN after the honest site-level card**
  - Why it matters: Repetition makes the paused state feel more broken than intentional.
  - Fix: Specify a compact stale-table treatment without hiding route coverage.
  - Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-time visitor):** The primary path is clear, but a NO sentence beside an UNKNOWN card can make the verdict feel unreliable.

**Casey (mobile visitor):** The 390px viewport has no horizontal overflow and keeps status plus human explanation above the fold. Full primary navigation is still absent in the header.

**Sam (keyboard/screen-reader visitor):** Search and link targets retain visible focus and semantic labels. Status remains text-first, not color-only.

### Minor Observations
